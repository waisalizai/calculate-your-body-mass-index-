Calculate your body mass index (BMI) for adults

Use this calculator to check the BMI of an adult aged 18 and over. However, you should not use it to diagnose any symptoms . If you are worried about your weight, contact your local pharmacist or GP surgery.

Please do not use this tool if you are under 18 years old, pregnant, have been diagnosed with an eating disorder, or think you may have one and have a condition that affect your height.

It is noteworthy that the BMI calculation is just one measure of health; however, it cannot tell the difference between muscle and fat.
